Banana Walk

Privacy policy

Banana Walk turns your steps into coins and collectible bananas. This page says what the app keeps, where it goes, and how to get rid of it.

Effective 19 September 2026
1Your steps stay on your phone unless you sign in. The app reads them from the phone's motion sensor and from Apple Health, and never sends them anywhere on its own.
2Signing in is optional. It exists so your progress can follow you to a new phone. Sign in and a copy of your game save is stored under your account.
3No ads, no analytics, no tracking. Nothing in the app profiles you or sells anything about you.
4Delete Account in the app removes your account and the cloud copy of your save at once.

What the app collects

Without an account, everything below lives only on your phone, in the app's own storage, and leaves with the app when you delete it.

DataWhere it comes fromWhy
Step counts and walking distanceYour phone's motion sensor, through Apple's CoreMotion, after you allow Motion & Fitness accessTo count today's steps, credit coins, and keep your daily step history and personal records
Daily step totalsApple Health, read only, after you allow Health access. This is how steps counted by an Apple Watch reach the app. The app writes nothing to Health.To make each day's total match Health when the phone was not carried
Game progressCreated by playingCoins, pulls, your banana collection, streaks, quests, achievements, mileage and records
SettingsChosen by youDaily goal, text size, sound, haptics, reminder time
Banana Club statusThe App Store, when you subscribeTo unlock membership features. Apple handles payment; the app never sees your card or billing details

The app does not read your location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone. From Health it reads step count only, and only when you allow it; no other Health data is read, and nothing is written. It asks for Motion & Fitness and Health access the first time it needs steps, and for notification permission only if you turn the daily reminder on. Reminders are scheduled on the phone itself and are not sent from a server.

If you sign in

You can sign in with Apple, Google or a phone number from the You tab. Sign-in is handled by Firebase Authentication, a Google service. When you do, the app receives and stores:

  • A user id created for you by Firebase.
  • The name and email address the provider shares. With Sign in with Apple you can hide your email, and the app works fine with the relay address Apple gives it.
  • Your phone number, if you sign in with one. Firebase sends the verification code by SMS.

A copy of your game save, the progress and settings listed above, is then stored in Cloud Firestore under your account, together with your total step count. It is uploaded when it changes and when the app goes to the background. When you sign in on another phone, the app offers to restore that copy. Cloud data is only readable by the signed-in account that owns it.

Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore are operated by Google LLC. Their handling of that data is described in Google's Privacy and Security in Firebase. The app has Firebase Analytics and advertising features switched off.

The home-screen widget

The widget shows your equipped banana, today's steps and the coins toward your next pull. It reads a small snapshot the app writes into a storage area shared only between the app and the widget on your phone. Nothing about the widget leaves the device.

What the app never does

  • Show ads or use advertising identifiers.
  • Run analytics, crash reporting or any tracking SDK.
  • Sell, rent or share your data with anyone other than the providers named above, and only as far as sign-in and cloud backup need them.
  • Collect data from children on purpose. Banana Walk is not directed at children under 13, and no account is needed to play.

Deleting your data

  • On this phone: delete the app. All local progress goes with it.
  • In the cloud: open the You tab, then Account, then Delete Account. This removes your Firebase account and the stored copy of your save immediately. Sign Out alone keeps the cloud copy so you can come back to it.
  • Motion access: can be withdrawn at any time in iOS Settings, Privacy & Security, Motion & Fitness. The app then stops counting steps.
  • Health access: can be withdrawn at any time in the Health app under Sharing, Apps. The app then counts only what the phone's own sensor sees.

Changes

If the app starts handling data differently, this page will be updated and the date at the top will change. Changes that matter will also be mentioned in the app's release notes.
